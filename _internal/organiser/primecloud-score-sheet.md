## Scale and arithmetic

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Not demonstrated |
| 1 | Very poor |
| 2 | Below expectations |
| 3 | Meets expectations |
| 4 | Very good |
| 5 | Excellent |

Weighted score = (Judge rating / 5) × Criterion weight.
Sum the five weighted values for this category's total out of 100.
There are no formulas or automatic rankings in this template.

- Review completed independently:

Organisers manually average complete, valid, non-conflicted sheets for this
category only. Judges have equal weight; missing marks are not zero.
Require at least two valid independent reviews per contender, preferably three.
Compare unrounded averages. For a tie, the non-conflicted panel reviews evidence
against the approved category criteria and reaches consensus; no extra criteria.
Do not combine this total with an AI-category or local overall score.
Keep completed sheets, provisional results and deliberations access-controlled.

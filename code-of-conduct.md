## Spirit & applicability

The **Hacktoberfest Hack Day Nairobi × iTech Club** is dedicated to providing a safe, inclusive, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of gender, gender identity and expression, age, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, religion (or lack thereof), or technical experience.

This event operates under the **[MLH Code of Conduct](https://github.com/MLH/mlh-policies/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md)** and the **[MLH Standard Hackathon Rules](https://github.com/MLH/mlh-policies/blob/main/standard-hackathon-rules.md)** in full. By registering, checking in, or attending, every participant, mentor, judge, sponsor representative, and organizer agrees to uphold these standards throughout the event.

---

## Summary of core expectations

We do not copy long policy text into this document, but summarize our collective commitments under the official MLH standards:

- **Treat everyone with respect and dignity:** Harassment, discrimination, derogatory jokes, intimidation, or disruptive behavior will not be tolerated in person, in chat channels, or in project code.
- **Foster an inclusive learning environment:** Support beginners and peers. An overnight hackathon is an educational space where questions are welcomed and mentorship is valued over gatekeeping.
- **Respect personal and physical boundaries:** Always obtain consent before taking photographs, recording video, or touching another attendee or their personal property.
- **Maintain technical and academic integrity:** Build fresh during the hacking window, respect open-source licenses, attribute AI tools honestly, and do not plagiarize code or misrepresent functionality to judges.
- **Follow venue and safety instructions:** Comply promptly with directions from university security, venue staff, and iTech Club organizers.

For the comprehensive policy, read the [official MLH Code of Conduct](https://github.com/MLH/mlh-policies/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md).

## Reporting an incident or concern

If you experience or witness harassment, feel unsafe, or have any concern during the event:

1. **On-site iTech Club Organizers:** Approach an organiser or event mentor immediately. Ask for the person handling conduct and safety concerns.
2. **Contact Event Host:** Message the lead organizer directly via the **Contact Host** link on the [official MLH event page](https://events.mlh.com/events/14735-hacktoberfest-hack-day-nairobi-x-itech-club).
3. **Report to MLH Directly:** Contact Major League Hacking directly via email at `incidents@mlh.io` or through the contact methods detailed in the [MLH Code of Conduct](https://github.com/MLH/mlh-policies/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md).

Share only the information needed to report the concern. The official MLH policy explains reporting and enforcement.

---

## Enforcement & consequences

Reports are handled under the official MLH Code of Conduct and applicable venue safety procedures. Follow directions from event staff and university security. Conduct violations can lead to removal from the event or competition under those rules.

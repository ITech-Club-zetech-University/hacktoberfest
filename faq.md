# FAQ

#### Do I need to be an expert in AI/ML to take part?

No. All 5 challenges are designed to be approachable with the [Starter Kit]({{ site.baseurl }}/starter-kit/) and a bit of help from mentors. Calling Gemma through the Gemini API is just an API call — you don't need to understand model internals to build something good.

#### Can I come without a team?

Yes — solo hackers and pairs are welcome at the event generally. To enter one of the 5 preset challenges (and compete for the iTech Club prizes), you'll need to form or join a team of 3–4 during the first 30 minutes. See [Rules → Team size]({{ site.baseurl }}/rules/#team-size).

#### Can my team enter more than one challenge?

Yes, if your project genuinely qualifies for more than one. Select every eligible challenge when you submit on MLH OrganizerHQ.

#### Does my repo need to be public during the hack, or just at submission?

It needs to be public (and stay public) by the submission deadline, and must **remain public after the event** to stay eligible for prizes — this follows the standard MLH rule. There's no rule against working in a private repo overnight and flipping it public before you submit, but don't leave it until the last minute.

#### What if we don't finish?

Submit anyway and demo what you have. Completion is one part of the [score sheet]({{ site.baseurl }}/judging/#the-score-sheet), not the whole thing — a broken-but-ambitious project can still score well on problem framing, use of Gemma, and learning.

#### Can we use a model other than Gemma?

The **iTech Club challenges on this site are Gemma-specific** — Gemma (any size/variant) needs to be an important part of your project. For MLH's broader Best Open-Source AI Project challenge, any open-source or open-weight model qualifies, but to also win one of our 5 tracks, it has to be Gemma.

#### Is there internet at the venue, and should I rely on it?

Assume Wi-Fi may be congested with everyone downloading things at once. See [Starter Kit → Prerequisites]({{ site.baseurl }}/starter-kit/#1-prerequisites-checklist) — download model weights and dependencies **before** the event, and consider [Challenge 2]({{ site.baseurl }}/challenges/02-edge-offline/) if you want to sidestep connectivity entirely.

#### What counts as "an important part" of the project for Gemma?

If you removed Gemma, would the project still basically work? If yes, it's probably not an important part. Judges are specifically checking for this under the "Use of Gemma" criterion — see [Judging]({{ site.baseurl }}/judging/#the-score-sheet).

#### Who do I ask if I'm stuck on a rule or a submission question?

Find any organizer at the venue, or see [Resources → Where to get help]({{ site.baseurl }}/starter-kit/#where-to-get-help).

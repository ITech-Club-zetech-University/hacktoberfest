# Hacktoberfest Hack Day Nairobi x iTech Club
{: .fs-9 }

An overnight, open-source hack day exploring AI and working software, hosted by **iTech Club, Zetech University**, in partnership with **Major League Hacking (MLH)**.
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

**Identify a real problem. Imagine a better solution. Build it with open-source AI. Explore what Gemma 4 makes possible. Publish your work for others to use and improve.**

[Build Your Own Project]({{ site.baseurl }}/challenges/){: .btn .btn-primary .fs-5 .mb-4 .mb-md-0 .mr-2 }
[Register on MLH](https://events.mlh.com/events/14735-hacktoberfest-hack-day-nairobi-x-itech-club){: .btn .fs-5 .mb-4 .mb-md-0 }

## Your problem. Your idea. Your implementation.

There are **no fixed challenges or mandatory tracks**. Choose your users, problem, domain, product concept, model and technical approach. Aim for one useful, demonstrable MVP rather than an unfinished commercial platform.

Use the [seven-step idea framework]({{ site.baseurl }}/challenges/#develop-an-idea-quickly), copy the [problem statement worksheet]({{ site.baseurl }}/templates/problem-statement/), or browse [optional Project Inspiration]({{ site.baseurl }}/inspiration/). You do not need to choose an example.

### Best Project Deployed on PrimeCloud

**Sponsor: [PrimeCloud](https://primecloud.cloud). Build. Launch. Grow.** Launch an original application with public licensed GitHub source and a working public PrimeCloud deployment. AI is optional.

**PrimeCloud Production Plan – 3 Months**  
The winning developer will receive three months of PrimeCloud Production, with access to the standard Production plan features and allowances.

[Requirements and deployment evidence]({{ site.baseurl }}/challenges/#best-project-deployed-on-primecloud)

These are **award categories, not project prompts or technology tracks**. Submit once to every category your project qualifies for. Gemma 4 is required only for its category; PrimeCloud deployment only for its category. DigitalOcean remains optional. See the [category requirements]({{ site.baseurl }}/challenges/#the-official-mlh-challenge) and [submission steps]({{ site.baseurl }}/submission/), including what to do if a category is missing from the form.

**Judging is human-led.** Judges independently assess each eligible category using the [published judging guide]({{ site.baseurl }}/judging/). Each category is considered separately; Gemma 4 is required only for its own award.

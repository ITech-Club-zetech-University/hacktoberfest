# Project Inspiration

These are **optional starting points**, not prescribed challenges, required domains or MLH-approved tracks. You do not have to select one. Adapt, combine or ignore them, and choose your own model and architecture according to the [project brief and prize requirements]({{ site.baseurl }}/challenges/).

Start with a user and a small outcome, not a feature list:

| Area | A possible focused starting point | A useful demonstration |
|---|---|---|
| Education and learning support | Help a learner understand one mistake in their attempted solution rather than completing assessed work for them. | Compare a helpful hint with the expected concept; handle an ambiguous attempt. |
| Agriculture, food systems and climate resilience | Help a grower find relevant guidance in a small set of public, dated extension bulletins. | Cite the source and date; admit when a question is not covered. Do not invent forecasts or safety-critical advice. |
| Accessibility and assistive technology | Explain a photographed public notice in a format a user can access. | Check dates and locations against the image; handle unreadable text and offer human verification. |
| Local-language tools and communication | Draft a short bilingual community announcement with editable terminology. | Ask a speaker to review meaning and flag uncertain translations rather than claiming universal language accuracy. |
| Community services and civic information | Help residents locate requirements in a small collection of official service documents. | Show citations and document dates; distinguish information from legal advice. |
| Small-business productivity and operations | Turn synthetic stock notes into a proposed reorder checklist. | Validate quantities and require a person to approve actions; do not place orders automatically. |
| Developer tools and open-source maintenance | Help a maintainer understand a reproducible test failure. | Link explanations to actual test output; bound tools and separate suggested fixes from approved changes. |
| Creative work and media understanding | Help a creator organise their own permitted images with editable descriptions. | Compare descriptions with the source media; disclose uncertain interpretations and respect consent/copyright. |
| Research and knowledge discovery | Help a researcher compare claims in a small collection of openly licensed papers. | Cite passages, distinguish evidence from synthesis and surface conflicting or missing evidence. |
| Health information organisation or education | Help someone organise public health-education leaflets by topic and source. | Use synthetic/public data, show authoritative sources and uncertainty, and direct clinical questions to qualified professionals. **No unsupported diagnosis, triage, treatment or personalised medical claims.** |

For any idea, identify one user, one working flow, test scenarios and a failure boundary using the [problem statement worksheet]({{ site.baseurl }}/templates/problem-statement/). Identify an important AI role if entering an AI category.

**Non-AI projects are welcome for [Best Project Deployed on PrimeCloud]({{ site.baseurl }}/challenges/#best-project-deployed-on-primecloud).** For example, build a small booking application, community-resource directory, inventory API or developer tool and demonstrate it at a working public PrimeCloud URL. These are optional ideas, not new tracks. An AI application may also enter PrimeCloud when independently eligible.

Technical patterns such as retrieval, local inference, multimodal inputs, reusable agent skills and bounded developer tools may help, but none is compulsory. An effective small CLI can be as valuable as a web or mobile app.

## Choose your own direction

These examples are not an eligibility checklist. Use the [current project brief]({{ site.baseurl }}/challenges/) and [rules]({{ site.baseurl }}/rules/) to plan your original project.

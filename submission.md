# Submit Your Project

Publish your project, prepare a working demo and submit through the official event page. The [idea canvas]({{ site.baseurl }}/templates/problem-statement/), [README template]({{ site.baseurl }}/templates/project-readme-template/) and [demo guide]({{ site.baseurl }}/templates/demo-script/) help you prepare; they do **not** replace the submission form.

## Where and when to submit

Open the event's [Challenges area](https://www.mlh.com/events/hacktoberfest-hack-day-nairobi-x-itech-club/challenges), then sign in to submit. The public link may first open the event overview; use its challenge/submission controls.

Both official category pages display a submission close of **Saturday, 31 October 2026, 6:00 AM EAT**:

- [Best Open-Source AI Project](https://www.mlh.com/events/hacktoberfest-hack-day-nairobi-x-itech-club/challenges/01a0d987-0c41-12ea-13ac-5b85755dc2f3)
- [Best Use of Gemma 4](https://www.mlh.com/events/hacktoberfest-hack-day-nairobi-x-itech-club/challenges/01a03b87-be22-9180-5e2d-b3beeb66666d)

**One submission can enter every category it qualifies for.** Use one project and repository, selecting all applicable categories. DigitalOcean is optional. Category entry does not guarantee eligibility or multiple prizes.

## Submit and check your entry

The [official MLH submission guide](https://hacktoberfest-handbook.mlh.com/project-submissions-and-judging-for-hacktoberfest-hack-day) describes this workflow. Follow the current form if its wording differs.

1. **Register and check in.** One team member submits on behalf of the team using their registered account.
2. **Open the event's Challenges area.** Choose **Add Submission** during the submission window. If the control is missing or access fails, ask staff before the deadline.
3. **Describe your project.** Provide its name, public GitHub repository link, description and technologies used. Include a demo URL if available.
4. **Select eligible categories.** Choose each applicable award under **Which challenge(s) are you submitting to?** Meet the [requirements for each category]({{ site.baseurl }}/challenges/#the-official-mlh-challenge).
5. **Save and verify.** Choose **Submit Project**, then reopen the saved entry. Check the repository URL, project details, team information and entered categories.

Complete any additional fields required by the actual form. A README, idea canvas or unsent form alone is not a submission. Do not assume a grace period or an alternative submission channel if access fails.

## What to have ready

- **Public source and licence:** your working code and an appropriate open-source code licence.
- **Useful README:** problem, users, original contribution, architecture, exact model/variant, inference route, model terms, dependencies, setup and tests.
- **Honest evidence:** working features, actual test results, limitations, data sources and permissions, safety/privacy considerations and team contributions.
- **Working demonstration:** one clear user journey, the real AI contribution and a limitation or edge case. Keep secrets and private data out of demos.
- **Category evidence:** meaningful open-source/open-weight AI for the broad award; actual Gemma 4 integration and the published API conditions for the Gemma award.

Use the [pre-submission checklist]({{ site.baseurl }}/templates/submission-checklist/). Keep the repository public after submission and follow the event briefing for your demo slot.
